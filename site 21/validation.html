03  —  Does It Actually Work?

Validation

Tested against three real MLB pitchers, chosen specifically for their different, well-known reputations. Each pitch is graded across four models — whiff, hard-hit, ground ball, and barrel rate — plus real platoon splits by batter handedness. Every grade below is relative to the entire league, not just these three pitchers: 100 is league average, higher is better, lower is worse.
