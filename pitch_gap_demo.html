Pitch Shape vs. Performance

Where "stuff" and results disagree

A model trained on 116,355 real MLB pitches predicts expected whiff rate from shape alone (velocity, spin, break, extension). Comparing that prediction against actual results — and against location data — separates two different problems: is a pitch underperforming because of its shape, or because of where it's being thrown?
